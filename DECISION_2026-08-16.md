the middle portion of the range."* "Middle portion" carries no number, so I swept

**Every interpretation loses, and the incumbent is the only positive one.** The
conclusion does not depend on my guess at the threshold, which is what makes it
safe to act on.

Mechanism: the source wording classifies far more sessions as TREND (864 vs 660
across four symbols), and TREND is the weaker bucket. It is a looser test wearing
a stricter one's clothes.

**Caveat, stated plainly.** This rejects a rule the trader supplied, on in-sample
evidence. It is reversible and should be revisited if the sealed period disagrees.

## DECLINED — BIAS TREND

The source is explicit: *"Bias is supplied by the user; you never invent it."*
A setup against the bias is not a trade.

Our engine **derives** direction from bias (`close_location >= 0.50`), so direction
always equals bias and the filter can never fire. The engine has no NO-TRADE state.
**Roughly half of the 1,030 trades measured are not trades under the source
strategy.** What was measured is an unfiltered superset.

I am not inventing a bias rule. This project already has one invented rule on
record — the market-fill fallback, worth a fake +6.25R, which survived in the spec
until the trader asked where it came from. The provenance tags exist because of it.
Inventing a bias rule now would make every subsequent number unfalsifiable in the
same way, and it would contradict the source directly.

**What I can do instead is specify the job precisely.** A filter can only remove
trades, and removing trades at random changes expectancy by exactly zero. So the
bias read must be genuinely skilful, and here is how skilful:

```
ex-gold, efficiency ratio:  767 trades, win 23.6%, avg win +3.762R, avg loss -1.123R

to reach  +0.00R/trade  ->  discard  0% of losers (already there)
to reach  +0.10R/trade  ->  discard  7.5% of losers while keeping ALL winners
```

**7.5%.** That is the entire remaining question. If your bias read can eliminate one
loser in thirteen without costing a winner, the strategy clears the expectancy gate.
If it cannot, no configuration in this study reaches it.

That is a testable claim the moment a bias series exists.

## Defect found and fixed during this run

`fetch_mt5_year.py` wrote prices with `repr(float(x))`, producing sixteen decimal
places of float artifact (`1.1614100000000001`). `load_master` infers POINT from the
decimal count, inferred `1e-16`, and **every spread cost rounded to zero** on EURUSD
and GBPUSD — 524 trades reported gross in the net column with no warning.

First run: −43.204R. Corrected: −117.683R.

Fixed three ways: `repair_precision.py` (lossless — the digits were never in the
quote), the fetcher now formats from `symbol_info().digits`, and `load_master` now
raises on more than 8 decimals rather than inferring nonsense. The Oct-2022 fixture
still reproduces at exactly +27.124R, so the engine is unchanged.
